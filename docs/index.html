Features

Everything in its place.

NutKeeper handles all the tedious sorting so you never have to dig through a pile of files again.

🗂️

Smart type sorting

Files land in Images, Videos, Documents, Audio, Archives, and Other — automatically detected by file extension.

📅

Date-based subfolders

Inside each category, files are organised into year and month subfolders based on creation or modification date.

📁

Folder archiving

Pre-existing subfolders are kept untouched and moved to a dedicated "Preexisting Folders" directory.

👁️

Preview mode

See exactly what will happen before anything moves. Review every file's planned destination in the log.

🔒

Duplicate-safe

Files are never overwritten. Name collisions are resolved automatically — photo_1.jpg, photo_2.jpg…

⚡

Instant & offline

No cloud, no account, no internet. Runs entirely on your Mac and processes thousands of files in seconds.

How it works

Three steps to a clean folder.

Designed to be fast and obvious — no configuration, no learning curve.

1

Choose a folder

Click Browse and pick any folder on your Mac — Downloads, Desktop, an external drive, anywhere that needs a tidy-up.

2

Preview the plan

Hit Preview to see a complete breakdown of every file and where it will go. Nothing moves until you say so.

3

Organise

Click Organise Files and confirm. NutKeeper moves everything instantly and shows live progress in the activity log.
